# Smoking Health Risk Analysis Dashboard | Power BI

## Project Overview

The **Smoking Health Risk Analysis Dashboard** is an interactive business intelligence project developed using Microsoft Power BI. It explores patterns between smoking habits, patient demographics, organ conditions, and selected lifestyle and health indicators.

The project focuses on transforming raw data into meaningful visual insights through KPI cards, interactive charts, slicers, and organ-specific report pages.

## Objectives

- Analyze the distribution of smoking statuses.
- Explore smoking habits across different demographic groups.
- Compare recorded healthy and damaged organ conditions.
- Examine lifestyle indicators such as alcohol consumption and family history risk.
- Present health-related data through an interactive, visually consistent dashboard.

## Tools & Technologies

- **Microsoft Power BI Desktop** — dashboard development and visualization
- **Power Query** — data preparation and transformation
- **DAX** — calculations and KPI measures
- **CSV datasets** — source data

## Dashboard Pages

1. **Smoking Health Overview** — key metrics, smoking-status distribution, organ conditions, and health indicators.
2. **Human Body & Organ Analysis** — organ selection, condition filters, and anatomical visuals.
3. **Heart Analysis** — smoking-related indicators and selected health metrics.
4. **Human Body Analysis** — demographic and health-indicator visuals.
5. **Kidney Analysis** — selected health metrics and visual analysis.
6. **Liver Analysis** — selected health metrics and visual analysis.
7. **Lungs Analysis** — smoking habits and lung-related visual context.
8. **Risk Factor Analysis** — smoking status by gender, smoking duration, alcohol consumption, organ conditions by age, BMI by blood pressure risk, and family history risk.

## Key Analytical Areas

- Smoking-status distribution
- Average cigarettes consumed per day by smoking status
- Organ-condition comparisons
- Demographic analysis by age and gender
- Average years of smoking
- Alcohol-consumption categories
- BMI across blood pressure risk categories
- Family history risk distribution

## Skills Demonstrated

- Data cleaning and preparation

## Important Interpretation Note

This dashboard is an exploratory analysis of the available dataset. Observed patterns and associations do not establish that smoking caused a particular health condition. The dashboard is not a medical diagnostic tool and should not be used to make individual medical decisions.
